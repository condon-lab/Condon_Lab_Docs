# Submitting the Manuscript 

## Writing a cover letter 
A cover letter is sometimes optional or required. This is basically like a job cover letter describing why this paper is a good fit for the journal. 

You want to make sure your cover letter includes: 

1. Summarize the contents of your paper very briefly (1-4 sentences) 
2. Describe the impact and importance of this paper/research and how it connects with the aim and scope of the journal 
3. Thank the editor for their consideration 

## Upload documents and submit 
Make sure when submitting, you are uploading the correct format, either .docx or .pdf file. 


Congrats on submitting your paper.🙂 The waiting game begins. You should have received an email once you submit. This email will have information on how to track the status of your paper. Routinely check it every other week. If a considerable amount of time has passed since submitting, and the status has not changed, email the editor and ask for an update. Keep Laura updated on progress, and she can advise on next steps.
